examples

One capability each, small enough to read in a sitting. Every page reports which backend the browser actually gave it, and every switch changes the running scene, building a new renderer in place where a quality option needs one. Three keep their behaviour in DriftScript; save the script while the page is open and the page carries on with it.

  • starter A lit cube on a ground plane, in ninety lines. The one to copy out of the repository when beginning a project.
  • your first game A complete 3D game: an arena, a character, crates to push and orbs to gather against the clock, with shadows, sound, pause and a HUD. The manual walks through it.
  • a 2D game Breakout, complete with paddle, bricks, score and lives — laid out in the XY plane with the camera parked down the Z axis. There is no 2D mode, and none is needed.
  • ambient occlusion and bloom The post chain, and why bloom needs hdrScene beside it to find anything at all.
  • antialiasing sceneSamples at 1 and at 4, on near-vertical edges — the case that looks broken without it.
  • 2D overlay and text Screen-space panels and the built-in font, a keycap prompt, and a portrait in a box of its own.
  • world-space lines Strokes with width in metres, an antialiased edge, and a buffer rewritten in place rather than reallocated.
  • materials Image maps built in code, metalness and roughness, and the per-draw surface dials.
  • instancing Ten thousand rocks in batches that cull as a whole and rock by rock, and a batch rewritten every frame.
  • lights Lamps that cast, a spot throwing a window through a cookie, a photometric downlight and a window that is an area light.
  • lightmaps A room lit by light baked once, with a box's soft shadow no shadow map draws, read from a page at each surface's second coordinates.
  • DriftLight Seven hundred candles: the sixteen nearest shaded exactly, the rest summed into an occluded volume.
  • DriftRay Bounced light traced on the GPU, following a wall as it changes colour. WebGPU.
  • light in the air A sunbeam through a window, fire and smoke, and a medium that fills the room with light.
  • sky and atmosphere A day in ninety seconds: sun, moon, stars and clouds, and lamps that come on at dusk.
  • fog and weather Fog, one wind for everything, rain, lightning and puddles that mirror the street.
  • water The open sea under three winds, a planar reflection, and caustics under a pier.
  • colour and motion Exposure, a colour grade, vignette, grain, a fade, depth of field and motion blur.
  • DriftTR The scene drawn smaller and rebuilt to full size by temporal reconstruction. WebGPU.
  • GPU-driven pipeline Two and a half thousand meshes culled and drawn by the device. WebGPU.
  • particles Sparks that streak, smoke that rises and thins, and motes, from deterministic pools.
  • picking Hover and click: what is under the pointer.
  • terrain A valley two kilometres across drawn as a clipmap, painted and planted by a DriftScript module.
  • large worlds A town three thousand kilometres out, streamed in cells by where the camera will be, with walkers that freeze and thaw.
  • hierarchical detail A city of 144 blocks, each drawn at the level its distance deserves, with crossfades between levels.
  • worlds from a kit A street of thousands of copies of three pieces, carried as placements and expanded into a mesh.
  • procedural solids A gatehouse built in code from closed solids, with windows and a doorway cut by booleans.
  • gaussian splats A rock garden of sixty-six thousand splats, sorted off the frame, with a post of ordinary geometry standing in it.
  • drifttexture Materials as small programs decoded per pixel, one of them animated by the simulation's clock. WebGPU.
  • writable textures A wall a scripted turret scorches, with a clock that runs backwards and burns the same marks again.
  • driftcapture A clip of a room fused into a surface, proposed as floor and scenery, with balls dropped onto it.
  • rigid bodies A wall of crates, things rolling down a ramp, and a cannon scripted in DriftScript, with the world's fingerprint.
  • joints A bridge of hinged planks, a door on a motor, a swinging lamp and a chain that breaks.
  • queries and colliders A laser that asks the world what it meets, a sensor square that counts what stands on it, and a sliding wall.
  • characters A character to walk about a valley, over a bridge and under it, up steps, off a slab and into a pond.
  • vehicles A raycast car on a ring of cones, driven by a DriftScript driver or by you, on tarmac or ice.
  • ragdolls and cloth A figure shoved down the stairs, a flag in a gusting wind, and a sheet dropped over a crate.
  • a garment A figure walking a circle in a cape: skinned cloth solved on the device, a finer mesh bound to it, the scene's wind.
  • animation A figure walking a circle on a blend tree, reaching for a lantern, its pace set in DriftScript.
  • navigation Villagers walking lanes in DriftScript and dogs crossing a square on a navigation mesh built from its geometry.
  • entities Frogs catching fireflies over a pond, every rule a DriftScript system, with time that runs backwards.
  • chemistry A campfire where everything heats at its own rate, with rain, wind and green wood scripted in DriftScript.
  • agents Villagers whose routines shelter from the rain and resume, and a crier who asks a model where to go next.
  • models A turntable that reads glTF, OBJ and STL in a worker and streams them in, or any model dropped on the page.
  • audio A courtyard heard from where the camera stands: a scripted bell, a cart behind a house, a crypt's reverb, and lanterns on the kick.
  • input A puck driven by keys, a gamepad or a thumb, with a controls screen that rebinds and saves, prompts for each pad, and rumble.
  • cinematic A buggy lapping a track with a jump, filmed by a scripted director, a cinematic written as data, and a keyed path.
  • sprites A garden of tiles and a gardener walking it, picking flowers, all one sprite pass, scripted in DriftScript.
  • interface A pause menu and an options screen over a running scene: a tree laid out, themed, clipped and scrolled, its behaviour in DriftScript.
  • xr A headset seen across a room: its head, controllers and tracked hand read every frame from a WebXR session, and a lamp a script turns up with the trigger.
  • saves A garden bed sown and grown in DriftScript that saves to a server that can be slow or offline, loads across a change to its components, and keeps the player's preferences.
  • recording A show of pillars and chimes kept three ways: a still, four seconds recorded as they play with the mix, and four seconds rendered frame by frame with a score.
  • display A video settings screen over a test card, each control drawn from what the host says it can do: window mode and size, orientation, safe area, wake lock, quitting and files.
  • tools Six bouncing balls and the tools overlay on F3: an inspector on the selected ball, a console of what the balls do, a profiler of the frame, and edits that undo.
  • netplay Two players in one match over a link that is slow and loses packets: each peer guesses the other's input, rewinds when it guessed wrong, and stays in step.
Served by npm run examples.
